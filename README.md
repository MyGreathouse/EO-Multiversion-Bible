Five translations. One powerful Bible. Read, compare, search, study, highlight, and reflect — beautifully designed, privacy-first, and built to work offline.
EO Multiversion Bible brings together five complete, public-domain translations — the Berean Standard Bible, World English Bible (British Edition), American Standard Version, Webster's Bible, and Young's Literal Translation — so you can read side by side and see how each renders a passage. Every verse links to hundreds of thousands of related cross-references, making it easy to trace a theme across the whole of Scripture. Bookmark what matters, highlight in colour, leave your own notes, and everything stays exactly where you left it — searchable, organised, always at hand.
No account to create, no data collected, nothing to sync. Once loaded, it works entirely offline, so Scripture is there whether you're online or not. A calm, distraction-free reading experience, designed to feel less like an app and more like a well-made book.
