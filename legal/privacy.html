The EO Multiversion Bible

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, favourites, reading history and preferences stay on your own device — we never see them, store them, or send them anywhere. The app is supported by advertising, which is a separate system from your reading data and never has access to it. This page explains exactly what that means.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, favourites, reading position, reading history, reading plan progress and display preferences are written to your browser's local storage on the device you are using. They are never uploaded, never synchronised to a server, and never visible to us or to any advertising partner.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using the Erase everything control in Settings, permanently deletes all of it. There is no server copy to recover, so use Export your study first if you want to keep it.

Advertising

The EO Multiversion Bible is a free app supported by advertising, served through Google AdSense. This is the one part of the app that does involve a third party, so we want to be specific about it rather than vague.

  • Advertising may use cookies, device identifiers, local storage or similar technologies, depending on your settings and location, to serve and measure ads.
  • Where required by law, we ask for your consent before any advertising or measurement technology is used, through a certified consent management platform. Advertising that requires consent is not requested until you have given it.
  • You can review or change your advertising choices at any time in Settings → Privacy & Advertising → Change privacy choices.
  • Advertising never appears inside the Scripture reading column — only in the clearly labelled areas set aside for it.
  • If you are offline, no advertisement is requested or shown. The Bible itself is entirely unaffected either way.

Advertising and your personal reading data are handled by separate systems in this app. Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, favourites and reading history are never sent to, used by, or shared with the advertising system.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or telephone number.
  • We do not read, transmit or analyse your personal notes.
  • We do not build a profile of what you read for our own purposes.
  • We do not sell your reading data, because we do not hold any.

Requests made to our servers

When you open the app your browser downloads the application files and the Scripture text you are reading. As with any website, our hosting provider receives standard technical request information (such as an IP address) in order to deliver those files. We do not use that information to identify you and we do not combine it with anything else. Once a book has been downloaded it is cached on your device and read offline.

Children

The EO Multiversion Bible is suitable for all ages. The core reading experience collects no information from anyone, including children. Where advertising is shown, it is not personalised to any user known or reasonably believed to be under 18.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have rights over personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about your Bible reading on any server — you already hold and control all of it directly on your device. For anything handled by the advertising system, you can exercise your choices through Settings → Privacy & Advertising, and Google's own privacy policy explains how it processes data on its side.

Contact

The EO Multiversion Bible is published by Elias Okafor. Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@risten.co.uk.
