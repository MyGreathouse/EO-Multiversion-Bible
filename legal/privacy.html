EO Multiversion Bible

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026.

The short version

EO Multiversion Bible has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you save stays on your own device. We do not collect, transmit or store your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, favourites, reading position, reading history and display preferences are written to your browser's local storage on the device you are using. They are never uploaded, never synchronised and never visible to anyone else.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using the Erase everything control in Settings, permanently deletes all of it. There is no server copy to recover, so use Export your study first if you want to keep it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or telephone number.
  • We do not read, transmit or analyse your personal notes.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels or third-party cookies.
  • We do not build a profile of what you read.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not hold any.

Requests made to our servers

When you open EO Multiversion Bible your browser downloads the application files and the Scripture text you are reading. As with any website, our hosting provider receives standard technical request information (such as an IP address) in order to deliver those files. We do not use that information to identify you and we do not combine it with anything else.

Once a book has been downloaded it is cached on your device and read offline.

Advertising

The current version of EO Multiversion Bible contains no advertising and no advertising software. If advertising is introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated first, the advertising provider will be named here, and any choices available to you will be described. Advertising will never appear inside the Scripture reading screen.

Children

EO Multiversion Bible is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have rights over personal data held about you. Because EO Multiversion Bible holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete on your behalf — you already hold and control all of it directly on your device.

Contact

EO Multiversion Bible is published by Elias Okafor. Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@risten.co.uk.
