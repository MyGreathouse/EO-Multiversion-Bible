Frequently asked questions

Why does the app show advertising?

The EO Multiversion Bible is free, with no account and no subscription. Advertising is what makes that possible — it supports the cost of keeping the app running and available to everyone, without charging readers.

Is the app local-first?

Yes. The Bible text, your bookmarks, highlights, notes, favourites and reading history are designed to stay on your own device wherever that's practical, and the core reading experience works without a server. Advertising is a separate system — it's the one part of the app that involves a third party, and it never has access to your personal reading data.

Does the app use cookies?

The reading experience itself does not. Advertising and its related measurement may use cookies, device identifiers, local storage or similar technologies, depending on your settings and location. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent before any of that happens.

Can I refuse advertising consent?

Yes. Where consent is required, you have a genuine choice to accept or decline — declining is just as easy as accepting, and nothing about the Bible itself changes either way.

Can I change my choice later?

Yes, any time. Go to Settings → Privacy & Advertising → Change privacy choices.

Can I use the Bible without an internet connection?

Yes. Once a translation has loaded, reading, searching, switching translations, comparing, bookmarking, highlighting, notes and reading history all continue to work fully offline. Advertising simply doesn't apply while you're offline — no ad is shown and nothing about your reading is affected.

Will declining advertising stop me reading the Bible?

No. Every part of the reading experience — every translation, every book, search, notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading plans — works exactly the same whether you accept or decline advertising. Scripture is never placed behind an advertising choice.
