Scripture licensing and attribution

Last updated 16 August 2026.

EO Multiversion Bible includes only translations that may lawfully be redistributed inside an application. Each is reproduced without alteration.

BSB — Berean Standard Bible

The Berean Standard Bible has been dedicated to the public domain by Bible Hub. It may be reproduced, distributed and used commercially without permission or royalty. No restriction applies to its inclusion here.

WEB-BE — World English Bible, British Edition

The World English Bible, British Edition (2020) is in the public domain, dedicated as such by Michael Paul Johnson and eBible.org. It carries no copyright restriction anywhere in the world.

ASV — American Standard Version

The American Standard Version (1901) is in the public domain worldwide. Its original copyright, held first by Thomas Nelson & Sons and later the International Council of Religious Education, has expired.

Webster's Bible

Noah Webster's 1833 revision of the King James Version is in the public domain worldwide; its publication date places it well beyond any applicable copyright term.

YLT — Young's Literal Translation

Robert Young's translation (1862, revised 1898) is in the public domain worldwide.

Translations that are not included

Copyrighted translations, including the NIV, ESV, NLT and TLB, are deliberately excluded. A translation will only be added to EO Multiversion Bible once a written licence has been obtained from its rights holder covering app distribution, offline caching, the territories concerned, and any commercial or advertising-supported use.

The King James Version is currently held back pending permission. Outside the United Kingdom the KJV (1769 Blayney revision) is public domain. Within the UK it is covered by perpetual Crown letters patent, exercised by Cambridge University Press as the Crown's patentee; free use there is capped at 500 verses for non-commercial purposes, well short of the complete text. Rather than distribute the full text to a UK-reachable, potentially advertising-supported service without that permission, it has been removed from this release. A request has been made to Cambridge University Press's Permissions Department; the KJV will be reinstated if and when written permission is granted.

Cross-reference data

Cross references shown alongside each verse come from OpenBible.info's crowd-sourced, community-ranked cross-reference dataset (357,057 links after this app's own integrity checks removed a small number that didn't resolve to a real verse). Unlike the Bible translations above, this dataset is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution licence, not dedicated to the public domain, so it carries a requirement to credit the source. That credit appears in the cross-references panel itself and here.

Typefaces

Literata and Archivo are used under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.

Reporting an error

If you find a discrepancy between EO Multiversion Bible's text and the published source of a translation, please report it to admin@risten.co.uk so it can be corrected against the source.
